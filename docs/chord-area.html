Chord Area

A = r² * (cos⁻¹((r-h)/r) - ((r-h)/r) * √(2rh - h²))

A = Area
r = radius
h = height of sagitta

The sagitta is the line representing the shortest distance form the circle's origin to the line that intersects with the circle. This line will aways always be perpendicular to the line of intersection.
