About Gabriel's Horn

Gabriel's Horn is a fascinating mathematical object created by rotating the curve y = 1/x around the x-axis for x ≥ 1. This creates a horn-shaped surface with a remarkable property:

Volume = π ∫₁^∞ (1/x)² dx = π [finite]
Surface Area = 2π ∫₁^∞ (1/x)√(1 + 1/x⁴) dx = ∞ [infinite]

This paradox suggests you could fill the horn with paint (finite volume) but could never paint its surface (infinite area) - hence "The Painter's Paradox".
